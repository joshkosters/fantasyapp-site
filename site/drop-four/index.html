Drop
Four.

Four in a row, with a few new tricks. Draft power discs, pick a world with its own twist, and line up four before your friend does. Free in your browser, one device, no account.

2 PLAYERS OR SOLO

Same board.
New tricks.

Everything you know about four in a row still works. Then the powers come out.

01
Power discs

Before each game you draft three powers each. A bomb that clears a hole, an anvil that flattens a column, a wild that counts for both of you.

02
Six worlds

Each world has its own discs, board, sound and an optional rule twist. Or design your own.

03
A CPU to beat

Five opponents, from Dot to Quad, each with their own style and something to say. Beat them to unlock worlds.

Pick a
world.

The look is only half of it. Turn on a world's twist and the rules change too.

Classic

No twist. Just the board you grew up with.

Deep Space

Black holes swallow the first disc dropped through them.

Arcade

Fill the bottom row and it clears, Tetris style.

Haunted

Discs fade from sight a few turns after they land.

Gridiron

Lines score touchdowns. First to three wins.

Tabletop

Walnut and stone. No twist.

Six ways
to play.

Mix any rules with any world. In a best of three or five, the loser picks the next rules.

01ClassicLine up four, across, down or diagonally.
02Pop OutDrop a disc, or pop one of yours out of the bottom row.
03Gravity FlipEvery sixth move the board turns upside down and everything falls.
04RoadblocksFive neutral stones start on the board.
05Five AliveA bigger 9 by 7 board where you need five in a row.
06MayhemEvery fourth move the board deals a surprise: a quake, a meteor, a turncoat disc.
