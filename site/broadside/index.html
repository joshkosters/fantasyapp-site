Broad
side.

The fleet battle you grew up with, plus eight ways to break it. Drifting ships, hidden mines, sonar and airstrikes. Free in your browser, one device, no account.

▶ Play now↗
2 PLAYERS OR SOLO

Same fleet.
New tricks.

Call your shots like always. Then the specials come out.

01
Special weapons

Sonar counts the ship squares in a 3 by 3. Airstrikes hit three in a line, clusters hit a plus, and torpedoes run a row until they find something.

02
Four worlds

Navy, deep space, pirates and arcade, each with its own ships, colors and lettering.

03
Three captains

Ensign Pip guesses. Captain Reyes hunts. Admiral Kraken plays the odds. Your record against each stays on your device.

Eight ways
to play.

Mix any rules with any world, with or without special weapons.

01ClassicOne shot a turn. Sink the whole fleet.
02SalvoOne shot for every ship you still have afloat. Lose ships, lose firepower.
03Hot StreakEvery hit earns another shot.
04DriftEvery three rounds, untouched ships slide a square. Old misses go stale.
05Fog of WarYour miss markers fade after three turns. Remember where you shot.
06MinefieldFour hidden mines in each sea. Hit one and it blasts your own fleet.
07Big SeaA 14 by 14 ocean and seven ships per side.
08MayhemEvery three rounds an event hits both fleets: meteor storms, spy planes, riptides, supply drops.
