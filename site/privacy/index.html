Privacy.

Last updated November 7, 2026

The short version

fantasyapp.io is a small site made by one person. The only thing we ask you for is your email address, and only if you want to hear when a new game launches. We show no ads and do not sell or share your email.

What we collect

If you sign up, we store your email address and the time you signed up. That is all. Playing the demos and games needs no account, and nothing you do in them is saved to our servers.

Why we collect it

To send you a short email when a new game launches, about once a week. We use it for nothing else.

Where it is stored

In a Firebase (Google Cloud Firestore) database that only the site owner can read. Our website hosting is Cloudflare, which, like any host, sees your IP address and basic request details when you visit.

Third parties

Some pages load fonts or images from other services, such as Google Fonts and ESPN team logos. Those services can see your IP address when your browser fetches them, as with any website. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for simple, cookieless traffic counts (pages viewed, country, device). It does not track you across sites or build profiles. We run no advertising trackers.

Email

If you sign up for updates, your email address is stored with Google Firebase and Buttondown, the service we use to send the newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we only use your address to tell you about new games.

Leaving the list

Ask to be removed and we will delete your email. Use the contact address below.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: hello@fantasyapp.io

Changes
