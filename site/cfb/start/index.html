Fantasy CFB / Start your own league

Start your
own league.

Always wanted a fantasy league, but for college football? You can run one for your friends, free. Setup takes about 30 to 45 minutes and you will need a computer.

Get the code on GitHub

Look under
the hood.

Real screens from the app, in a demo league with made-up teams. Swipe through.

Six steps.

Comfortable with a terminal? Easy. The full written guide is in the code's README.

01Make a free Firebase projectGo to console.firebase.google.com, create a project, turn on Google sign-in, and create a Firestore database. It holds your league. The free plan is plenty for friends.
02Download the codeGet it from GitHub (button at the top). It is one web page plus a few setup files. Nothing to build.
03Add your email as commissionerRun one short command that puts your Google email into the league config. You become the commissioner who runs the draft.
04Publish itInstall the Firebase tool, sign in, and run firebase deploy. You get your own web address for the league.
05Load the players and add your friendsOpen the admin page on your new site, load the Big Ten, SEC and Big 12 players, and add each friend by their Google email.
06Draft, then play every SaturdayFriends claim a team, you start the draft, and on game days you tap one button to pull the stats and score the week.
