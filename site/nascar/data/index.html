The driver list, qualifying order, finishing points and laps led, already in the format the Admin tab wants. Refreshed after every race.

Driver
list.

Paste into Admin > Drivers and press Import drivers. Do this before you open each week so tiers use fresh points. It follows the race you pick below, so you can also see the points as of any archived week. Format: name, number, team, points, wins, color, make.

Race
archive.

Every week is saved here, newest first, and the list grows after each race. It starts with the final two races of 2026, and a full season builds up from 2027. Pick a race, then paste each box into the matching card in Admin for that week.

1 · Qualifying

Admin > Week qualifying > Save qualifying. Fastest first. Pole +5, Q2 +3, Q3 +1.

2 · Scores

Admin > Week scores > Save scores. Finishing order, then NASCAR points. Saving marks the week scored.

3 · Laps led

Admin > Week laps led > Save laps led. Only drivers who led. +5 for a lap, +5 more for the most.

Good to know: the app may list a few drivers as unmatched after you save. Those are part-time drivers outside your league, and that is fine. Numbers and names come from NASCAR's public feeds, which can be a few hours late after a race.
