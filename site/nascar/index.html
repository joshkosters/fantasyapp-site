Pick three.
Beat your friends.

A small-group game built for a few friends and a race every week. Nothing to install and no cap on trash talk.

A
Three tiers

Drivers are split by season points into the top 12, the next 12 and everyone else. You take one from each tier every week.

B
Draft every week

Pick order starts from last place in the standings, then snakes. No two players can take the same driver. Each pick has a 12-hour clock and the draft locks before qualifying.

C
Real points, plus bonuses

Your drivers score their actual NASCAR points. Add 5 for winning the pole, 3 for second and 1 for third. Leading a lap is worth 5 and leading the most laps is 5 more.

The weekly rhythm

Results post.
The draft opens.

As soon as a race's results are in, picks open for the next one. Check the matchup page to see everyone's drivers side by side, then watch the standings move once the checkered flag falls. Pages for the draft, matchup, standings, race recap, awards and an admin view are all part of the game.
