Pick three.
Beat your friends.

A small-group game built for a few friends and a race every week. Nothing to install and no cap on trash talk.

A
Three tiers

Drivers are split by season points into the top 12, the next 12 and everyone else. You take one from each tier every week.

B
Draft every week

Pick order starts from last place in the standings, then snakes. No two players can take the same driver. Each pick has a 12-hour clock and the draft locks before qualifying.

C
Real points, plus bonuses

Your drivers score their actual NASCAR points. Add 5 for winning the pole, 3 for second and 1 for third. Leading a lap is worth 5 and leading the most laps is 5 more.

The weekly rhythm

Results post.
The draft opens.

As soon as a race's results are in, picks open for the next one. Check the matchup page to see everyone's drivers side by side, then watch the standings move once the checkered flag falls. Pages for the draft, matchup, standings, race recap, awards and an admin view are all part of the game.

Run your
own league.

It is one HTML file on a free Firebase project. About 30 minutes, and you will need a computer.

01Make a free Firebase projectCreate it at console.firebase.google.com, turn on Google sign-in and create a Firestore database. Register a web app and copy its config.
02Get the codeThe game lives in the fantasy-nascar folder on GitHub. Paste your Firebase config into index.html and set the commissioner email to your own.
03Deploy itRun firebase deploy in that folder to publish the page and the security rules. You get a link to send your friends.
04Add players and driversSign in as the commissioner and open the Admin tab. Paste in your players (email, name) and the driver list (name, number, team, points, wins).
05Open a weekPick the race and a lock time. Tiers and pick order are set for you, and players draft from their phones.
06Post the resultsAfter the race, paste qualifying order, finishing points and laps led. Standings, recap and awards update on their own.
